FAQ

Website design in the Sydney CBD: common questions

Do you build websites for professional firms in the Sydney CBD?

Yes. Professional-services sites are a regular part of the work, including the BRM Patent Attorneys site in the portfolio. CBD firms usually choose a multi-page site or a Custom Website on WordPress, with a page for each practice area so each service can be found on its own.

Should a city business use a website builder or a freelancer?

If you have a simple offer, a free weekend and you enjoy tinkering, a builder like Wix or Squarespace can be enough, and both are reasonable platforms. A freelancer makes more sense when your time is worth more than the build, when you need the site structured for local search from day one, or when a DIY attempt took all weekend and still looks off. In the CBD, where competitors often have agency-built sites, looking established on the first visit is part of the job.

With Website Builder Advice the starting point is a $500 one-page site, live in 1 business day, so the gap between DIY and done-for-you is smaller than most owners expect.

How fast can a Surry Hills or Pyrmont business get a site live?

A single landing page is delivered live in 1 business day once the brief is ready. A multi-page site (home, service, contact and thank-you pages) is live in 1 week. A Custom Website of 10 to 15 pages on WordPress is scoped with you before work starts.

Can my team update the website after launch?

It depends on the build. The $500 and multi-page sites are fast static builds (clean HTML and CSS, no WordPress or plugins), so changes are made for you on request and Marcelo stays your contact for updates. If you want to edit pages yourself, the Custom Website is built on WordPress, which gives you a content editor.

Will the site support our Google Ads and local search in the city?

Every build is mobile-first and fast-loading, with page headings, service copy and location signals structured for local search. Multi-page sites are connected to your Google Business Profile. For Google Ads, a single-purpose landing page usually converts better than sending clicks to a busy homepage. Nobody can honestly promise a ranking, but a clear, fast site gives Google and your customers less reason to look elsewhere.

Do you work with businesses across the CBD and inner city, or meet in person?

Yes, from Barangaroo and The Rocks to Surry Hills and Pyrmont. Most projects run by phone and email, with drafts reviewed online. If you would like to meet in the city, ask when you call 0404 084 847.

Does a city business website need to meet accessibility and privacy rules?

Builds use mobile-first layouts, clear headings, readable contrast and labelled forms, which are core parts of WCAG 2.2, the accessibility standard most Australian guidance points to. If your site collects names, emails or phone numbers, it needs a privacy policy, and the OAIC's guide to the Australian Privacy Principles explains when those principles apply to a business.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check your obligations with a qualified adviser.
