FAQ

Website design in Sydney: common questions

Which parts of Sydney do you work in?

Website Builder Sydney is based in Hurstville and works with businesses across St George and Bayside, Canterbury-Bankstown, the Sutherland Shire and the Sydney CBD and inner city. Parramatta projects run through FP Web Design. Past clients also include businesses on the Northern Beaches, in Wollongong and in Melbourne, so distance is rarely a problem.

Should I use a website builder like Wix or Squarespace, or hire a freelancer?

If you have a simple offer, a free weekend and you enjoy tinkering, a builder like Wix or Squarespace can be enough, and both are reasonable platforms. A freelancer makes more sense when your time is worth more than the build, when you need the site structured for local search from day one, or when a DIY attempt took all weekend and still looks off.

With Website Builder Sydney the starting point is a $500 one-page site, live in 1 business day, so the gap between DIY and done-for-you is smaller than most owners expect.

How long does a small-business website take?

A single landing page is delivered live in 1 business day once the brief is ready. A multi-page site (home, service, contact and thank-you pages) is live in 1 week. A Custom Website of 10 to 15 pages on WordPress is scoped with you before work starts.

Can I update the website myself after launch?

It depends on the build. The $500 and multi-page sites are fast static builds (clean HTML and CSS, no WordPress or plugins), so changes are made for you on request and Marcelo stays your contact for updates. If you want to edit pages yourself, the Custom Website is built on WordPress, which gives you a content editor.

Will my new website be ready for Google and work well on phones?

Every build is mobile-first and fast-loading, with page headings, service copy and location signals structured for local search. Multi-page sites are connected to your Google Business Profile. Nobody can honestly promise a ranking, but a clear, fast site gives Google and your customers less reason to look elsewhere.

Do we need to meet in person?

Usually not. A short brief or a phone call is enough to get started, and the draft is reviewed online before anything goes live. If you would rather talk it through face to face, ask when you call 0404 084 847.

Is my website accessible and compliant with Australian privacy rules?

Builds use mobile-first layouts, clear headings, readable contrast and labelled forms, which are core parts of WCAG 2.2, the accessibility standard most Australian guidance points to. If your site collects names, emails or phone numbers, it needs a privacy policy, and the OAIC's guide to the Australian Privacy Principles explains when those principles apply to a business.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check your obligations with a qualified adviser.
